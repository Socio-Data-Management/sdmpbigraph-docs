Legend

The Legend card lists the series (the threads of the web). It is hidden automatically when there is only one series, and with one radar per legend value, where each radar already names its series in its title. A legend value listed once keeps its color in every radar of a grid.

PropertyDescriptionDefault
Show LegendShow or hide the legendOn
PositionTop, Bottom, or Left / Right aligned to the top, centre or bottomTop
FontFamily, size, bold, italic, underlineSegoe UI 11
Assign series color to font colorPrint each label in its series colorOff

Positions​

Top and Bottom lay the legend out horizontally across the full width, centred. The four side positions stack the entries vertically in a column capped at 150 px wide, aligned to the top, the middle or the bottom of the plot area. The web takes whatever space is left, and shrinks to keep its axis labels inside the viewport.

Logos​

Logos come from the Logo field, read per legend value. The same field also feeds the axis-end logos, read per axis.

PropertyDescriptionDefault
Legend DisplayText, Logo and Text, or LogoText
Text WrapEllipsis (one line, ends with …) or Wrap (flows onto several lines). Never scrolls.Ellipsis
Logo Width / Logo HeightSize of the logo in pixels20 × 20

The color swatch is always drawn, so the legend stays readable in Logo mode. Entries without an image fall back to text, so a partially filled Logo field still produces a readable legend.

Colors applied to the axes

When the Radar card applies colors to the axes, every thread shares one color — the legend swatches are then all identical, which is expected: the series are told apart by their label or logo, not by their color.

Text Wrap​

A legend box is bounded: 150 px wide when it sits on the left or the right, the width of the visual when it sits on top or below. Text Wrap decides what happens to an entry that does not fit.

ModeResult
Ellipsis (default)The entry stays on one line and ends with an ellipsis
WrapThe entry flows onto as many lines as it needs

Neither mode ever produces a scrollbar. A scrollbar inside a report tile hides entries behind a control nobody thinks to drag, so it was removed outright.

Entries that no longer fit

With scrolling gone, a legend taller than the space it has is clipped rather than scrolled. Wrap makes entries taller and so clips sooner than Ellipsis — give the legend a horizontal position, or shorten the labels, when every series has to stay visible.

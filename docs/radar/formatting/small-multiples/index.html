Small Multiples

One radar crowded with a dozen threads stops being readable. Small multiples lay several radars out in a grid instead, all drawn on the same axes, so that each profile can be read on its own and compared with its neighbours.

What a radar is made of​

There are three ways to cut the data into radars. The first one that applies wins.

One Radar per = Measuremeasure of the Values wellone per legend valuethe measure name

With the Small multiples well filled, the One Radar per setting is hidden and a status line says that the field decides. Leave the well empty and set One Radar per to None to get back the single radar.

Two levels of breakdown

To get one radar per region with one thread per brand, drop the region in Small multiples and the brand in Legend. Putting both fields in the Legend well would give one thread per region × brand combination instead.

Properties​

PropertyDescriptionDefault
One Radar perNone, Legend value or Measure — hidden when the Small multiples well is filledNone
Max Charts per RowNumber of radars side by side, up to 53
Spacing (px)Gap between radars, horizontally and vertically12
Apply Logo Field toSmall multiples field only — Axes (axis ends) or Radar titlesAxes
Reference in Every RadarLegend value only — repeats the reference series in the other radarsOn
Hide Crowded LabelsDrops the data labels of a radar too small to hold themOn

Radars fill each row, then wrap; a partial last row is centred. Every radar has the same size, so a smaller web never reads as a smaller value.

At most 5 × 3 radars​

Past five radars in width or three in height, a web is too small to be read. When the data holds more than Max Charts per Row × 3 radars, the grid first widens, up to five per row. Beyond 15 radars, the extra ones are not drawn: a note at the bottom right of the visual says how many are missing (+4 radars not shown), and the pane repeats it. Filter the report, or split the visual in two, to see them.

Colors and legend​

A legend value keeps the same color in every radar, from the usual sources: the Data Colors card, then the Series Color field, then the report theme.

  • With One Radar per = Legend value, each radar is in the color of its series. The legend is hidden: every radar already names its series in its title.
  • In the other modes the legend lists the legend values once, however many radars repeat them.

Logos in the titles​

With the Small multiples well filled, the Logo field can give each radar the image of its value — a flag per country, a logo per planet. Set Apply Logo Field to to Radar titles, then pick Logo and Text or Logo in the Series Title card.

The field is read once per radar: the first image found among its rows. It must be a measure, or a column summarized as First, returning a base64 image for the value of the radar — typically MAX ( Planets[logo] ).

The switch exists because one field cannot do both jobs: left on Axes, a logo that varies from radar to radar would give each spoke the image of whichever radar came first. With Radar titles, the axis ends carry no logo. An entry of the Series logos (JSON) lookup still wins over the field for its title.

Scales​

Radars showing the same measure share their axes: the same value sits at the same distance from the centre in every radar, which is what makes them comparable. The Scale card still decides between one scale per axis and one scale shared by every axis.

Radars showing different measures — one radar per measure, or one radar per legend value without a legend — each get their own axes. A score and a share have no scale in common, whatever the Scale card says. The graduation style (ticks, web, circles) is unaffected: it applies inside each radar.

Manual bounds set with the gear icon apply to every radar.

Reference in every radar​

With one radar per legend value, the reference series used by the Significance card sits in a radar of its own, away from the series it is compared with. Reference in Every Radar repeats it as a grey dashed thread in each of the other radars, under the web, without fill, marker or tooltip.

The reference is the series matched by the Reference series (regex) of the first test set to vs. Reference series. The significance tests themselves are unchanged: every series is still tested against that reference, axis by axis, wherever the two are drawn.

Crowded labels​

A small web cannot hold as many labels as a large one. What crowds it is not only its size but the number of labels it carries: axes × threads. With Hide Crowded Labels on, the labels of a radar are estimated at their real length and laid end to end around the web; when they would go round it more than once, that radar is drawn without data labels. Radars that have room keep theirs.

The significance icons, texts and markers stay visible either way. Only the Label background view of the tests disappears with the labels it colors.

Animation​

With the Cascade type of the Animation card, the radars open one after the other. Grow opens them all at once.
